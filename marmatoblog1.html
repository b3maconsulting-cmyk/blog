AI is becoming a dealership-wide conversation

The supplied workshop agenda points to a broad set of priorities: AI search, service retention, digital retail, data quality and employee performance. Together, these themes suggest that the next useful AI conversation starts with a business problem and a clear way to measure improvement.

For dealers, that could mean following up on an enquiry sooner, making inspection videos more useful, or identifying customers who are ready to return for service. CRM and marketing automation can help connect these moments into a more consistent customer experience.

Start with one measurable customer journey.

Choose a workflow, establish its current performance, test an improvement and review the outcome before expanding.

Where to focus your attention

01 / Make your data usable

Review duplicate records, missing contact details and consent information before building automated audiences or AI-assisted workflows.

02 / Connect marketing to sales

Map the journey from campaign response to showroom visit. Agree on lead ownership, follow-up expectations and conversion measures.

03 / Build service relationships

Explore reminders, relevant service communications and inspection follow-up. Track appointments, return visits and customer feedback.

04 / Help your people perform

Pair new tools with clear processes and coaching. Let teams review customer-facing outputs and use their experience to improve the workflow.
